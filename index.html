About

I study how continents deform. My group combines structural geology, field mapping, and geochronology to reconstruct the histories of mountain belts and the sedimentary basins next to them. We use U-Pb geochronology to date rocks and trace where sediment came from, and (U-Th)/He thermochronology to work out when rocks were exhumed toward the surface.

This page collects my open, GitHub-based work: preprints, data, code and teaching materials. My work focuses on the Himalaya–Tibet (Xizang) orogen and on the western United States, including the fold-thrust belts and basins of Montana. In Montana we also apply this structural and basin-scale framework to energy and natural-resource questions.
